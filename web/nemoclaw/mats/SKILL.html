Companion-material provenance

Previewable source, license, and connection level for everything under web/nemoclaw/mats/. Click preview on any essay to lazy-load a local excerpt, and click any figure to enlarge it. The repo is Apache-2.0 (LICENSE.md): course-authored essays, cards, and index JSON are Apache-2.0; cached NVIDIA pages keep NVIDIA's ownership; arXiv summary cards are our own prose and link out to the paper's listing for its license. External sources are hyperlinks (their pages set X-Frame-Options and cannot be embedded in an iframe, so the link opens them in a new tab).

Connection levels. original: course-authored essay, guide, or index. summary: course-authored card citing a source, no source text reproduced. compilation: course-authored collection of source links. conversion: cached markdown mirror of a source page.

Companion figures (in mats/)

These two SVGs sit in mats/ rather than assets/figures/ because they are the paper figures kept beside their summary cards, not page diagrams. Both are unreferenced: mats/rag_arch_2021.svg is byte-identical to assets/rag_arch_2021.svg (a duplicate), and mats/graph_rag_2024.svg is a smaller variant of the assets copy. They are safe to remove in a cleanup. Figure provenance for the pages lives in assets/SKILL.html.
