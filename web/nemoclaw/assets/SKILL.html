Image provenance

Previewable source, license, and connection level for every image under web/nemoclaw/assets/. Click any preview to enlarge it. The repo is Apache-2.0 (LICENSE.md), and every SVG here is an original hand-authored redraw: nothing is copied pixel-for-pixel. The license column names the cited source's own terms (informational), and where a source states no reuse license it says so rather than presuming one.

Connection levels. recreation: faithful redraw of a specific published figure. inspiration: original drawing of a concept taken from a source. conversion: format change or mirror of an existing source asset. original: authored for this course, no external source figure.

Why some SVGs are not in assets/figures/. figures/*.svg holds the 19 themed diagrams (they ride var(--gfx-*) and are fetched + injected inline so they follow the light/dark theme). The root assets/*.svg are out of that pipeline for two reasons: three are baked-color <img> paper redraws still in use (hardcoded hex, because an <img>-loaded SVG cannot read the page's CSS variables), and five are unreferenced legacy standalones superseded by the inline figures (flagged in amber below; removable in a cleanup).
