Image provenance

Source, terms, and connection level for every image under web/nemoclaw/assets/. The repo is Apache-2.0 (LICENSE.md), and every SVG here is hand-authored for this course: nothing is copied pixel-for-pixel from a source. The "source terms" column names the cited source's ownership for reference only, since each figure is redrawn or cited rather than reproduced.

Connection levels. recreation: faithful redraw of a specific published figure. inspiration: original drawing of a concept taken from a source. conversion: format change or mirror of an existing source asset. original: authored for this course, no external source figure.
