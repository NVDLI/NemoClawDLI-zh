scripts/compliance source gates

Compliance tooling enforces release-source requirements for licensing, contribution terms, package evidence, and private URLs. Governed assets and materials must also carry provenance.

These checks run before legal review, which remains required.

How software is used and distributed

  1. Do learners receive it? Included in the course means yes. Choices such as Used only to check the course identify packages kept in authoring or CI environments.
  2. Why is it here? Where it comes from separates packages chosen directly from dependencies and task-specific tools. License or terms gives the recorded result. Needs source-specific review points to evidence and does not grant permission.
  3. Need a smaller list? Check several choices in one section to include any of them. Choices in different sections narrow the results together. Search narrows them again. Download what you see keeps the same rows and sort order. The container field only adds a spreadsheet note.

How sources and materials are used

  1. Was it shipped? What happened to the source? separates reference-only sources from anything shipped to learners. Its other choices identify author-only tools and distinguish recreated assets from copied material or browser code.
  2. What changed? How was it used? records whether the course summarized, converted, recreated, or imported it.
  3. Can I verify the row? License or source terms shows the recorded terms and links to supporting source/repository evidence. Unclear evidence stays unclear.
