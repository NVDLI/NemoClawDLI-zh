Homescripts

scripts/compliance source gates

Compliance tooling covers the repository's release-source posture: Apache-2.0 license, inbound contribution terms, reproducible package/license evidence acquisition, private URL sweep, and provenance coverage for governed assets and materials.

This is a pre-review gate. It prevents known bad states before legal review; it does not replace legal review.

Executable checks

Source gatepython3 scripts/compliance/source_gate.py
JSON outputpython3 scripts/compliance/source_gate.py --json
Third-party listpython3 scripts/compliance/third_party_inventory_audit.py --self-test && python3 scripts/compliance/third_party_inventory_audit.py
Post-scan licensespython3 scripts/compliance/render_sbom_license_inventory.py --sbom <cyclonedx.json> --artifact-id <digest-bound-artifact> --out <inventory.md>
Keep the static inventory compact. The complete npm, Python, source-tree, conditional image, material-provenance, evidence-retention, and attachment commands live in the SBOM generation runbook.
