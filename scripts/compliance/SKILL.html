Homescripts

scripts/compliance source gates

Compliance tooling covers the repository's release-source posture: Apache-2.0 license, inbound contribution terms, reproducible package/license evidence acquisition, private URL sweep, and provenance coverage for governed assets and materials.

This is a pre-review gate. It prevents known bad states before legal review; it does not replace legal review.
